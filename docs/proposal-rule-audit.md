# Proposal rule audit: Phigros Session 2026–2027

Source: **The Animation and Comics Association, HKU Phigros Competition Proposal of Session 2026–2027**, 32 PDF pages, supplied for this review. Page references below are the actual PDF page numbers. Every page was checked, including the image-only registration-form material on pages 25–28. This audit contains no participant contact details, payment QR codes or private staff information.

The normative competition description is in §16 (pages 11–14), §19 (pages 17–19), and Appendix A (pages 19–24). The later screenshots visibly describe an older Project Sekai event and conflict with the Phigros body; those screenshots are excluded as historical material. The table records the intended proposal-mode behaviour and the manual work that remains with organisers. [The English rules](competition-rules.md) present these decisions for competitors and operators.

## Explicit operational interpretations

These are stated interpretations where the proposal is incomplete. The mean-ACC method was confirmed for this event during implementation review. Other remaining defaults should be reviewed by ACA before competition day. None is attributed to wording that the PDF does not contain.

1. **Confirmed ACC comparison:** use the equally weighted arithmetic mean of each base song's original displayed ACC, rounded to four decimal places. The event organiser confirmed mean Accuracy and entry of original screen values. The proposal itself gives no aggregation method, weight or conversion formula. Missing ACC does not become zero. Do not derive ACC or game score using the old registration screenshot's judgement formula.
2. **Plan A combined seeds:** first-round qualifiers occupy seeds 1–4 in first-round order; revival qualifiers occupy 5–8 in revival order. The proposal does not define a combined rank between those different song sets. Main control can review a different ACA order before bracket scoring starts.
3. **Plan B labels/order:** A is odd-numbered and B even-numbered. The text's Group 1/2 and A/B descriptions do not consistently specify the parity mapping. Alternating A1/B1/A2/B2/A3/B3/A4/B4 seed order gives the specified cross-group opening pairs and a defined ordering for subsequent reseeding.
4. **Ban is optional:** “may ban one” permits an explicit pass. The draw waits for a decision from each competitor; it need not wait for two actual bans. Random picks come from the real remaining four, five or six candidates.
5. **Single rematch allowance:** the two one-rematch clauses share one allowance. Qualifiers count it per competitor, round and song; a different competitor's incident has its own allowance. Double elimination counts it per match song, shared by the two replaying opponents. The proposal does not explain whether the two causes can each grant a rematch. Each unconfirmed tiebreak round has its own single replay allowance under the same evidence rules. Changing scenes, redrawing or resetting must not erase prior authorisations. ACA retains authority over exceptional evidence problems.
6. **No Grand Final reset:** preserve the explicit fourteen-match, single-GF programme. The undefeated WB champion can therefore finish Second after their first loss in GF. The general two-loss sentence is not used to introduce a fifteenth match.
7. **Round-number corrections:** treat the six round headings as authoritative. W7 winner enters R6 GF, W7 loser enters R5 LB Final, and L6 winner enters R6 GF. The references to R5 GF/R4 LB Final are numbering errors.
8. **Qualification-boundary wording:** the generic “eighth place” sentence on p12 is applied through the more specific fourth-place decisions in Plan A's two rounds or Plan B's two groups. It does not justify combining different song-set totals.
9. **Unspecified small fields:** fewer than eight eligible qualifiers, both opponents disqualified, multiple vacancies or a vacancy after play begins require an ACA decision. No invented scores, automatic random winner or silent completed-field reassignment.

Highest-versus-lowest reseeding and six GF candidates are explicit Phigros body rules, rather than optional interpretations. The system's prior fixed cross-bracket rematch-avoidance pairing and eight-candidate finals are legacy behaviours.

Older fourteen-match diagrams that draw fixed R2/R3 opponent routes also require revision. The fourteen match count and six round headings remain valid, but the proposal's actual opponent routes depend on the original seeds of the entrants who reach each reseeded field. Use the live bracket's resolved routes for the current event rather than interpreting the older illustration as a fixed pairing rule.

## Legacy event compatibility

Existing schema-1 tournaments without an explicitly configured proposal plan/ruleset retain their former two-group qualification, fixed bracket and historical finals data. An already saved eight-candidate GF remains valid historical data. Upgrading software must not silently redistribute entrants, recalculate a completed winner, discard existing scores or overwrite the live `data/` folder.

Proposal rules are configured by the main operator before scoring/advancement of a new event. New score metadata can be optional when reading old scores; missing evidence cannot resolve a newly requested ACC tie. Staff must refresh upgraded control/OBS clients and review the actual active sheet before submitting scores. Separate scorekeeper sessions remain restricted to score entry.

## Excluded obsolete and non-rule material

The table of contents lists old appendix names; the actual page-25 Appendix B links this repository, and Appendix C shows a registration form. The form screenshots are visibly labelled **Project Sekai Competition 2026**, with older dates, fees, prizes and personnel. Pages 25–27 include the following incompatible rules, which are not applied to this Phigros event:

- PERFECT × 3 + GREAT × 2 + GOOD × 1 + BAD × 0 + MISS × 0 scoring; the Phigros body instead uses the game's total score including combo and has no GREAT category.
- Batches of four rather than the Phigros body's up-to-three entrants.
- JP/TW/EN versions and specified one-star character cards; these concern Project Sekai, not global-version Phigros.
- Two songs drawn directly rather than the Phigros body's six candidates, optional bans and final two-song draw.
- A two-song GF chosen outside the list, rather than six candidates with two audience songs and a third host song.
- Old GF ties drawing outside the song list; the Phigros body's §16.1.2 prescribes the DE song list and ACC first.

The repeated generic rules about score-screen retention, consent, respect, ten-minute absence, property damage and ACA authority agree with the Phigros body. They are included above because the body independently states them, not because the old screenshots define the new rules.

Promotional channels/timelines, staff assignments, budget, payment methods/QR codes, contact information, floor-plan images, prize-price references and the old form's unrelated personal-information fields do not define score calculation or bracket behaviour. They were reviewed but are not copied into the public rules or broadcast state. Weather postponement/refunds and registration closure remain organiser tasks. No automated messaging, payment collection or public disclosure of private proposal details is introduced.

## Operational boundary

The server can validate saved scores, restrict staff roles, keep round/match context, persist authorised draws and adjudications, and make recorded eligibility affect future paths. It cannot certify a physical device, inspect a game installation, recover a YouTube score, determine whether a departure was accidental, judge consent, detect physical absence or assess misconduct. Main control records those findings only after the responsible arbiter has made them. Score entry alone does not certify that a match was played fairly or that an actual OBS feed shows the named player.
