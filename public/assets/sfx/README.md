# Selection switch sound

`song-switch.ogg` is the complete `Tap3.wav` from the same community mirror of the user-provided Phigros 4.0.1 audio collection used by the bundled menu BGM. The mirror maps this asset to `ui.select`; this is a community mapping, not an official asset specification.

Source: https://github.com/DDZmumo/Phiguess/blob/3840ad52fbd121f90ed0dec69f29702114f58925/apps/web/public/audio/phigros-4.0.1/Tap3.wav

The unmodified source SHA-256 is `8630ab0741db6a80c2f4314158e47c970f5389ecd9842c242a26995178e9b1b1`, checked against the source manifest. The complete 0.457483-second WAV is encoded as Ogg Vorbis quality 5 with −11.6 dB gain, preserving pitch and speed and placing its peak at approximately −14 dBFS. Two active sound voices are allowed, with a 20 ms fade when an older voice retires; the shared master volume applies afterward. Staff previews remain silent. Scene exits, mute and page disposal stop the sound; missed ticks are never queued for later playback.
